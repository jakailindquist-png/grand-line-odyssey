Known differences from canon

  • The existing world mod advertises 194 destinations: 169 canon-inspired and 25 procedural destinations. Its coordinates, scale and recreations are a fan interpretation, not an official complete world atlas. Imported builds are supplied by that mod's author, who credits the One Piece World Project.
  • One Mine Piece World's navigation opens routes after five activities. Canon magnetic recording is time-dependent, and a normal Paradise Log Pose has one needle rather than three. The guide calls out this difference.
  • The mod provides a playable Laugh Tale finale through its route system. The pack does not invent the true One Piece treasure or claim this is canon. It does not currently replace that finale with a four-Road-Poneglyph puzzle.
  • Supply credits, the quartermaster and the 16 contracts are original pack gameplay. Credits are separate from Mine Mine no Mi's Belly and bounty values.
  • Small Ships vessels and Eureka block ships are not exact replicas of the Going Merry or Thousand Sunny. Players can build their own ships with Eureka.
  • Calm Belt hazards and Seastone coatings belong to the world mod. Protection for Small Ships/Eureka vessels has not been established; use the intended Reverse Mountain route rather than assuming a custom ship is immune.
  • Singleplayer/LAN includes exploration and the custom contracts. The world author's competitive conquest, unique-fruit and some progression systems are reserved for dedicated servers; e4mc does not convert an integrated world into a dedicated server.
  • The four campaigns contain 76 original player assignments. The Pirate path follows the broad order of the main voyage; alternate faction paths do not claim to be manga plots. Contacts and recurring rivals are original characters.
  • Choices adjust rewards, trust and reputation, but do not create 76 fully branching cinematic stories. Some exact bosses are absent from this version.
  • The title image is newly generated One Piece fan artwork, not an anime frame. See ARTWORK.md. Ships depicted there are art, not supplied replica schematics.

World-life expansion

  • Mt. Colubo and Curly Dadan: mountain-family setting and character appearance. The imported house identity remains a map inference.
  • Zeff: Baratie proprietor and hospitality.
  • Arabasta Saga animals, Water 7 Saga animals and Dressrosa Saga animals: regional creatures.
  • Alex’s Mobs and Upgrade Aquatic: author descriptions, compatible releases and creature features.

Side investigations, resident schedules, service prices, relief outcomes and tropical habitat choices are original gameplay adaptations. They do not claim canonical chronology or manga events.

Expanded story roster — checked October 15, 2026

Official character references: Arlong, Crocodile, Kaido, Big Mom, Magellan, Caesar, Doflamingo. Official search excerpts identify Magellan’s venom fruit and Caesar’s gas fruit; the boss abilities are original gameplay adaptations. Model availability was independently checked against the actual pinned game registries. The campaign guide labels condensed, retrospective and hypothetical fights rather than presenting them as literal canon outcomes.

Orochi’s official profile identifies the Yamata-no-Orochi fruit model. The official Egghead column discusses the Elders’ transformed appearances. Their new combat zones and defeatable health bars are Odyssey adaptations rather than an assertion of manga outcomes.
