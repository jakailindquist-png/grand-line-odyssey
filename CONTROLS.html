First-time controls — 0.4.0

Updating without losing personal controls

Fresh imports include this coordinated keyboard layout. Existing custom controls are preserved when you keep your old options file. Open O → Voyage → Controls & first steps → View / change controls to inspect your actual bindings; Escape → Options → Controls → Key Binds also works.

WASD movement, Space jump, Left Shift sneak, Left Ctrl sprint, F offhand swap, Q drop and T chat retain Minecraft defaults. Menu keys are kept away from the main ability keys. Shader shortcuts and an upstream debug key are unbound; shader settings remain in Video Settings. Cannon-entry's conflicting F shortcut is also unbound; assign it in Controls if you use that feature.

The two fruit systems have different controls. J opens the correct menu for your fruit; G/V/N are specifically for Mine Piece fruits. For Mine Mine no Mi, equip moves and enter combat mode before using 1–8. A key bind cannot bypass character creation, energy costs, mastery, cooldowns or unsupported moves.

Controlling is included for searching bindings. Context-sensitive duplicates, such as recipes and sails on R, are intentional. Mods may show 1–8 as duplicates because combat mode reuses the hotbar. Inventory Profiles Next also has its own configurable shortcuts; inspect its settings if you have changed that mod's defaults.

Updating without losing personal controls

The safest update imports 0.4.0 as a separate instance and copies your backed-up world. To preserve your own layout, also copy the old instance's options.txt into the new instance before launching. Then set O for the journal and move shader selection off O if those older settings conflict. Use the table above for any other changes you want. Do not overwrite your only copy of a customized options file.
