NPCs, rivals and optional local AI

PlayingNemesis-style rivalriesEnable AI on the Windows hostFirst passage

The custom NPC systems run on the world host. Friends need the same modpack; they do not need their own AI installation. No cloud subscription or API key is required. Ordinary authored dialogue, quests and memories work with AI off.

Playing

  1. Finish Mine Mine no Mi's character creation. Enter /story and choose a campaign. /story align synchronizes its native faction afterward if needed. Celestial Dragon maps to World Government; noble status is a custom campaign.
  2. /story shows the current chapter, destination, encounter and cargo.
  3. On dry ground, /story contact calls your named envoy. Right-click them. Contacts wait for five minutes and can be recalled; their identity and memories persist even when the temporary in-world representative leaves.
  4. Visit the destination during the active chapter. If it has a boss, use /story encounter in a clearing on that island, or defeat that same boss there naturally. Players on the relevant chapter who damage the boss and remain nearby at its defeat receive credit. Deliver cargo to finish the chapter.
  5. Choose relief or influence. Relief gives more contact trust and local goodwill; influence gives additional credits and faction standing. Trust 20 or goodwill 20 reduces supply prices by one credit; goodwill 50 reduces them by two, with a minimum price of one. Outfits use the same discount in /voyage wardrobe.
  6. Give eight bread or insult your contact. Both affect future greetings and trust, with a five-minute cooldown per action. /story say <message> speaks to the nearby envoy. Questions about missions, fruits and rivals have authored answers when AI is off.

Nemesis-style rivalries

/nemesis lists three original rivals generated for your character. Each has a stable name, rank, grudge, victory/defeat counts, a recorded scar and recent memories. They use existing Marine/pirate character models. Scars are narrative records and combat adaptations, not newly painted textures.

Challenge one on open land. A rival who loses escapes and remembers the damage: fire, projectile or direct player melee. In later encounters, that category inflicts 25% less damage. Ranks cap at five and increase health, damage and armor. Rivals who defeat you gain promotions. Crew members can help drive a rival off.

Defeating enemy-faction fighters raises grudges. Once a grudge reaches ten, rivals can ambush you on dry ground, checked once per minute with at least ten minutes between successful ambushes. Direct challenges have a five-minute cooldown. Only one loaded rival encounter per player is allowed; encounters expire after ten minutes. Story boss encounters expire after twenty minutes.

Offer /nemesis truce within twelve blocks of an active rival, carrying eight gold ingots. Payment ends the encounter and prevents their ambushes until you attack their faction again. Explicit challenges also break a truce.

Memories are separate for each player, survive death and save/reload, and retain the last twelve events per contact/rival plus lifetime battle counters. This is a bounded recurring-rival system, not a simulation of the private lives of every NPC in the installed mods.

Enable AI on the Windows host

  1. Install and open Ollama for Windows.
  2. In Prism, open this instance's Minecraft folder. Open grandline-tools and run Enable-AI.cmd. It downloads llama3.2:3b (about 2 GB) and enables the local endpoint only after that model is available.
  3. Enter the world, call /story contact, stand next to the envoy, and enter /story say What do you remember about me?.
  4. Responses marked [AI] came from the local model. If it is unavailable, authored dialogue appears and the game remains playable.

The helper is optional. Manual equivalent, after installing Ollama:

ollama pull llama3.2:3b

Then set enabled to true in the instance's config/grandline-ai.json:

{
  "enabled": true,
  "endpoint": "http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/generate",
  "model": "llama3.2:3b",
  "timeoutSeconds": 45
}

The config is reread on dialogue requests; a restart is not required. Set enabled back to false to disable it. Other installed Ollama text models can be selected by name. Running a model alongside shaders and physics needs extra RAM/VRAM; reduce graphics if necessary. Model quality and speed depend on the PC.

Only localhost HTTP endpoints with /api/generate are accepted. The service is not exposed through e4mc. The host's model receives the selected envoy's briefing, current objective, your recorded actions, trust, bounded recent conversation and your message. Account names, UUIDs, coordinates and other players' chat are not included in that context. Recent conversation is stored in the world save.

One AI request runs at a time, off the game thread, with a 20-second per-player cooldown and a 45-second default timeout. Output is plain text: it cannot run commands, distribute items, declare objectives complete or edit NPC memories. The authoritative quest system decides those outcomes. AI responses are fan roleplay and can still make lore mistakes; infinite uniqueness is not guaranteed.

The local HTTP integration is tested against a mock Ollama-compatible endpoint. A downloaded model's narrative quality and Windows performance require a host playtest. No model weights or Ollama binaries are redistributed in this pack.

References: Ollama Windows documentation, generate API, Llama 3.2 model page.

First passage

After joining a story, /story embark offers one passage to its first island and sets a harbor respawn. This requires the One Mine Piece world type and chapter one; after using it, navigate onward with your ship and Log Pose.
