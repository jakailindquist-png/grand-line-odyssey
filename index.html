Town barriers: mapped islands and saved towns protect their blocks against attacks and explosions. Normal mining, building and combat remain available.

Crafting fixes: three vertical gold ingots craft a chest key. Three vertical iron ingots craft the Mine Mine no Mi pipe; add one stick beside the bottom ingot for the Mine Piece pipe. Read the recipes, update details and installation guide →

Get playing

Choose your starting point.

  1. Install Prism Launcher for Windows. Sign in with your Minecraft Java account.
  2. Download the modpack

    In Prism, choose Add Instance → Import and select the downloaded .mrpack. If using the Windows ZIP, extract it first.

  3. Select 64-bit Java 17. Start with 6 GB allocated on a 16 GB PC; hosting may need 8 GB.
  4. Create a Survival world with World Type: One Mine Piece. Press J, choose Race, Faction and Fighting Style, then click Finish.

The launcher downloads about 555 MiB of mods and shaders, plus Minecraft and Forge. Windows gameplay still needs testing on your PC.

  1. Close Minecraft. Back up the entire world folder from saves, including its data and serverconfig. Keep your old instance.
  2. Download full 0.5.1 update

    Import this .mrpack as a new Prism instance. The full pack includes Alex’s Mobs, Citadel, Upgrade Aquatic and Blueprint from 0.4.0 plus the story expansion and personal crew rewards in 0.5.1. Use this import to keep every player on the same version.

  3. Copy the backed-up world folder into the new instance’s saves folder. To retain custom controls, copy your old options.txt before launching; otherwise use the new coordinated layout.
  4. Press O for quests and J for abilities. See Controls and World life. Give every friend this same full pack before hosting.

Your first session, without the guesswork.

Finish your character

Press J. Choose Race, Faction and Fighting Style, then click Finish. If a key conflicts, open setup through chat.

/voyage setup

Press O or Inventory → Quests, then choose Pirate, Marine, Revolutionary or Celestial Dragon in the Story tab. When you belong to a crew, the captain chooses its shared campaign.

/story
Browse all campaign chapters →

Complete the first Pirate chapter

Use Survival mode. Open O → Story, choose First passage to reach Dawn Island, collect 24 oak planks, and complete the Higuma and Alvida opening encounters shown in the journal. Choose Meet Makino and stay within six blocks of her. Helpers can choose Contribute supplies to deposit their share of the planks before the final aid or ambition hand-in. Use Track for an objective on screen. The commands below remain available.

/story embark
/story contact
/story finish aid

Same world. Different houses.

Host on your gaming PC

  1. Open your world.
  2. Choose Esc → Open to LAN → Start LAN World.
  3. Copy e4mc’s public address from chat and send it to friends.
  4. Keep the world open and your PC awake.

Friends join from anywhere

  1. Install the same pack and addon version.
  2. Open Multiplayer → Direct Connection.
  3. Paste the host’s current public address.

A home IP or local LAN port will not work across the internet. The session ends when the host closes the world.
