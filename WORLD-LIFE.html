Islands, residents and ocean life — 0.5.0

Existing buildings become inhabitedNamed landmark residentsFaction investigations and consequencesTropical islands and a living seaScope and settings

The 0.4.0 world-life foundation extends the population system to all 169 mapped destinations and adds building interiors, faction investigations, tropical animals and ocean wildlife. It uses the existing imported map. Cities are discovered as you explore; the entire world is not populated in advance.

The 0.5.0 update adds the expanded story cast and boss sequences. These chapter meetings are separate from the three fixed landmarks below.

Existing buildings become inhabited

Walk into a built room. The host checks for a solid safe floor, a roof, enclosing walls and evidence of construction. Suitable rooms receive two to eight residents gradually. Kitchens, workshops, studies and households get their own local labels. Pure stone caves, open beaches, blocked floors and water are rejected. No buildings are replaced and distant chunks are not force-loaded.

Indoor residents stay close to their room. Street residents retain daytime work, evening gathering, night rest and weather routines. Streets are surveyed in 64-block quarters across the island as players explore, without a lifetime neighborhood or inhabited-room cap. Nearby arrival budgets default to 96 outdoor residents within 96 blocks and a separate 24 indoor residents within 80 blocks. The system adds at most eight outdoor residents per visitor per second, two per quarter in a pass. Suitable buildings and walking space are still required; these settings do not promise a particular population on every island. Saved identities prevent repeated arrivals on chunk reload; deaths use the existing replacement delay.

Original humanoid models replace the generic villager appearance. Residents have walking/look animations, varied skin and clothing colors, hair, eyes, collars, belts, pockets, caps and work aprons. Named residents have additional styling, including Dadan's orange hair, Zeff's chef hat and Rayleigh's white beard and glasses. These are original Minecraft interpretations, not extracted anime models or an exact replica of every character.

Faction investigations and consequences

Right-click a resident, then find the island incident in O → Town. Choose your Story campaign first. An investigation has four steps: ask a second resident in the same room/neighborhood, scout a clear outdoor route 24–96 blocks from the contact, defeat three indicated opponents near that route, and return to the original neighborhood.

The journal can start a bounded skirmish when the scouted route has room. Marine campaigns face pirates or Dawn bandits; revolutionaries face armed Marine checkpoints; celestial delegations face pirates. Pirates face Dawn bandits, rival pirates around Baratie/Gecko/Conomi, and Marines elsewhere. Civilians and traders do not count. Spawned opponents expire after two minutes if left unresolved.

The return offers payment or local relief. Payment gives 36 credits, two emeralds and 60 XP. Relief gives up the credits for extra trust; both outcomes publish local news and unlock a one-credit island supply discount. This combines with the existing trust discount. These are original faction-adapted incidents, not reenactments of every manga scene. Twenty islands have authored context; other destinations use a shared community investigation template.

One incident may be active alongside the main campaign, town errand and voyage contracts. The native crew shares its stages, tracking and completion. Any member can contribute and report; only the captain can abandon it. Each contributing member earns the full credits, two emeralds and 60 XP; choosing relief donates the crew’s credits. Offline contributors collect saved personal rewards from O → Voyage → Crew reward inbox. Merely joining the crew does not qualify, and previously completed incidents are not paid again. World news and supply benefits affect the island, while personal trust remains personal.

Small indoor households need not contain all eight service professions. Their ordinary town errands can use the required professions elsewhere on the same island. The investigation's second witness still belongs to the original room/neighborhood.

Tropical islands and a living sea

Alex's Mobs 1.22.9 and Upgrade Aquatic 6.0.3 are included, together with Citadel and Blueprint. They supply their original models, animations, behaviors, items and natural spawning. The pack downloads them from their authors.

A separate bounded habitat system supplements natural spawning on the imported map, including existing explored chunks:

  • Tropical destinations such as Dawn, Conomi, Jaya and Amazon Lily can receive capuchin monkeys, toucans, parrots and shoreline turtles. Land animals require natural ground; turtles require nearby water.
  • Warm seas can receive tropical fish, lionfish, mimic octopuses and hammerhead sharks. General waters include flying fish, dolphins, lobsters, comb jellies and nautiluses.
  • Cold water profiles include salmon and orcas. Whales and giant squid are rarer and require at least 24 and 32 blocks of water depth respectively.
  • One Piece species retain regional placement: Lapahn on Drum; Yagara Bulls at Water 7; Fighting Fish near Dressrosa; spotted ducks, Kung-Fu Dugongs and Bananawani around Alabasta; Humandrills on Kuraigana; Sea Cows near Conomi; flying fish near Sabaody.
  • Original News Coo seabirds can appear around islands. Right-click one to open the local journal bulletin. It reports the world's existing news rather than inventing AI events.

The supplement attempts one animal at a time, stops at 18 nearby animals by default, and shares a ten-minute success cooldown per 64-block habitat cell across players. Failed placements wait a minute. It checks loaded terrain, water depth and collision space; it does not continually summon animals around every player. Natural mod spawning retains its own rules and limits, so 18 is not a universal cap on all mobs.

New coral or other terrain features from Upgrade Aquatic may require newly generated compatible terrain. Imported map regions and already explored chunks are not rewritten with reefs. The animal supplement works without resetting your save. Surface animals are excluded from the sky/underwater special locations and extreme heights by the supplement.

Scope and settings

This adds persistent residents, routines, services, regional creatures, crew investigations and local consequences. It does not yet simulate a global economy, autonomous naval wars, every canon character's timetable, or AI dialogue for every civilian. Optional AI remains attached to the existing story-contact system and is disabled by default.

Host settings live in config/grandline-towns.toml. Lower cityOutdoorResidentLimit, cityIndoorResidentLimit and nearbyWildlifeLimit on slower hosts. regionalWildlife=false disables supplementary animals; enabled=false disables new town population. Existing entities remain. Old neighborhoodsPerIsland, inhabitedRoomsPerIsland and nearbyResidentLimit values no longer restrict the new city system. Existing neighborhood IDs, residents and accepted errands carry forward. Keep client and host versions identical.
