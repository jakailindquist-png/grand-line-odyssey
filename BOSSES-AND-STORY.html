Bosses and story cast — 0.5.1

The story now includes 74 distinct boss entity forms across 94 encounter stages, connected to 83 chapters across four paths. The Pirate route has 42 chapters: the original 35 retain their IDs, followed by seven explicitly hypothetical endgame trials. Arlong, Crocodile and Kaido are part of a much broader arc roster.

New in 0.5.1: personal crew rewards

Each member who contributes earns the full credits, XP and ordinary equipment reward. Fruit items, fruit loot boxes and large ship items remain one shared crew payout to the hand-in member (or another unpaid contributor if they earned it before). Cargo is handed in once. Returning contributors collect saved rewards from O → Voyage → Crew reward inbox. Updating does not duplicate rewards from completed chapters.

Play through the journal

  1. Press O → Story, choose a campaign, and track the active chapter.
  2. Sail to its destination in Survival on Easy, Normal or Hard. The journal gives the island coordinates.
  3. Choose Meet to call the chapter character to safe ground near your camp. Right-click the character to reopen the journal. Large characters need an open courtyard.
  4. Choose Fight in a broad clearing. Only the next stage can start. Defeat it, then open the journal for the next opponent. Completed stages remain saved for your crew.
  5. Bring supplies within six blocks of the current chapter contact on its island. Each helper can choose Contribute supplies to deposit part of the cargo and record their contribution. The final aid or ambition hand-in uses the crew’s deposits plus any missing supplies in the finishing member’s inventory, and awards the chapter rewards.

The first Pirate chapter now includes separate Higuma and Alvida opening retellings on Dawn plus the original 24 oak planks. Prepare your character and combat controls first. The journal explicitly shows both fights.

Crew progress and existing worlds

The pack uses your native Mine Mine no Mi crew. Stages, chapter completion and the tracked objective are shared. Crew members can use one another’s current story contact. Crew members can make recorded partial deposits with Contribute supplies; one member completes the final hand-in. Each requested item is consumed only once, and the journal counts your inventory plus saved crew deposits. Each eligible contributor receives the full credits and 80 XP, plus their own copy of ordinary chapter gear. Fruit items, fruit loot boxes and large ship items are a single shared crew payout, normally to the member completing the hand-in; the journal distinguishes them. If that member already earned this quest reward, an unpaid contributor receives the shared item instead. Choosing ambition adds four credits for every contributor. Contact trust and reputation remain personal to the member making the choice.

Contribute to quest objectives to qualify; crew membership alone is not enough. A contributor who is offline when the quest finishes can collect the saved reward later from O → Voyage → Crew reward inbox, even after changing crews. Each member can collect a payout only once. Completed chapters from before this update do not award another payout.

A victory requires an eligible survival crew participant and the matching spawned chapter encounter. Natural mobs, another crew’s boss, old scenes and repeated stage kills cannot skip the sequence. Only one encounter can remain active for the crew, including while its chunk is unloaded. It expires after 20 minutes of world time; a failed or environmental kill gives no progress and permits another attempt after the cooldown.

Existing completed chapters and recorded boss victories remain complete after updating. The new stages apply to unfinished boss objectives. This prevents an update from revoking earned progress. The seven appended trials become available after the original Pirate ending.

New custom bosses

Doflamingo, Caesar and Magellan were absent as usable bosses in the pinned mods. Odyssey now supplies original Minecraft-scale models and combat implementations for them.

  • Doflamingo: pink feather coat, sunglasses, melee attacks and a marked string-snare area. Step outside its circle during the two-second warning to avoid damage and slowing.
  • Caesar: horned scientist with a gas-colored mantle, melee pressure and a marked poisonous cloud. Move away from the smoke before it resolves.
  • Magellan: horned warden uniform and wings, melee pressure and a marked venom area with stronger poison.

The roster also adds Spandam (a weaker masked commander with a sword-sweep challenge), Orochi (an original eight-headed serpent), and beast forms for Saturn (horned spider), Mars (winged bird), Warcury (tusked boar), and Ju Peter (segmented sandworm). Their marked attacks use poison, knockback, slowing or suction. Nusjuro retains his native mod model, completing the five Elder encounters in the retrospective Egghead trial. These are defeatable gameplay adaptations; canonical regeneration is not simulated.

Each has a boss bar and three health phases. Later phases shorten attack intervals and increase damage. The marked attacks affect the encounter owner’s nearby crew, respect line of sight, and make no terrain edits or explosion sounds. These are original playable adaptations, not full replicas of every canonical technique. Native bosses retain their installed mods’ combat and visuals.

Every meeting has original before/after dialogue in the Story journal. The Marine, Revolutionary and Celestial paths keep their own assignments and faction choices. A familiar character appearing as a contact is a fan-created meeting adaptation, not a claim that they canonically served that faction. Optional local AI remains text-only and cannot award items or advance a quest.

Canon boundaries and remaining gaps

The Magellan trial, the reconstruction of Jack’s raid, the condensed emperor battles and the appended admiral/Blackbeard/Imu/Gunko trials are clearly labeled adaptations. A combat victory does not rewrite the manga’s escape scenes, deaths or unresolved ending. Smoker remains a contact at Loguetown rather than a required canonical defeat.

This release does not yet implement every named antagonist: the full Donquixote officer roster, Skypiea’s priests and the remaining Blackbeard captains still need dedicated encounters. Several supporting contacts use simplified original humanoids. Boss balance, every native transformation, Windows performance and a full crew playthrough have not been acceptance-tested. The public roster makes these gaps explicit rather than treating registered names as finished content.

Install

Back up your world and import the 0.5.1 mrpack as a new Prism instance. Copy your backed-up save into it. Give every friend the same version; the journal includes the new personal reward inbox. The full pack includes the wildlife dependencies added in 0.4.0. Keep the previous instance until you have checked the new one.
