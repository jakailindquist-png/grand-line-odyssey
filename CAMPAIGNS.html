Campaign reference

Pirate — 42 chaptersMarine — 15 chaptersRevolutionary Army — 14 chaptersCelestial Dragon — 12 chapters

All dialogue and player assignments below are original fan adaptations. Canon events are context, not a claim that every manga scene is simulated. Spoilers through Wano and the arrival at Egghead/Elbaph.

Pirate — 42 chapters

Follow the Straw Hats’ main route from Dawn to Elbaph through exploration and boss retellings.

1. A sail from Dawn

Begin at Dawn Island. Luffy starts his journey in East Blue; your own crew follows that route without replacing him. Secure timber for a first ship. Higuma and Alvida are separate opening combat retellings at this starting destination.

Destination: starter_east_blue. Encounter sequence: minepiece:higuma, mineminenomi:alvida. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:oak_planks. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:straw_hat + 8 credits.

2. The axe over Shells Town

Morgan rules the base through fear. Investigate the town and challenge his story encounter, echoing the liberation of Zoro.

Destination: shells_town. Encounter sequence: mineminenomi:morgan. Delivery: 8 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:zoros_shirt + 8 credits.

3. Orange Town under siege

Buggy threatens the settlement on the Organ Islands. Break his hold over the harbor in a retelling encounter.

Destination: organ_islands. Encounter sequence: minepiece:cabaji, mineminenomi:buggy. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 8 credits.

4. The liar and the captain

Usopp warns Syrup Village of a real threat. Face Kuro and help keep the village supplied.

Destination: gecko_islands. Encounter sequence: minepiece:sham, minepiece:buchi, minepiece:jango, mineminenomi:kuro. Delivery: 12 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:kabuto + 10 credits.

5. A meal on the Baratie

The sea restaurant feeds the hungry, even dangerous strangers. Face Don Krieg after gathering provisions.

Destination: baratie. Encounter sequence: minepiece:gin, mineminenomi:don_krieg. Delivery: 12 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:flintlock + 10 credits.

6. The promise at Arlong Park

Nami asks her friends for help. Challenge Arlong in the Conomi Islands and support the villages he exploited.

Destination: conomi_islands. Encounter sequence: minepiece:kuroobi, minepiece:hatchan, mineminenomi:arlong. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

7. The town of the beginning and end

Loguetown stands on the Polestar Islands. Resupply before entering the Grand Line; the canon crew escapes Smoker here rather than defeating him.

Destination: polestar_islands. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 8 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:sandai_kitetsu + 12 credits.

8. Across Reverse Mountain

Sail through the world mod’s Reverse Mountain route to Whisky Peak on Cactus Island. Baroque Works hides behind a welcoming harbor.

Destination: cactus_island. Encounter sequence: minepiece:ms_valentine, mineminenomi:mr_5. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 12 credits.

9. A hundred-year duel

Dorry and Brogy’s duel is interrupted by Baroque Works. Confront Mr. 3 in this prehistoric wilderness.

Destination: little_garden. Encounter sequence: mineminenomi:mr_3. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 12 credits.

10. Snow over Drum

Nami’s illness brings the crew to Drum. Help the island’s people and face Wapol, whose name is spelled Waporu in Mine Piece.

Destination: drum. Encounter sequence: minepiece:waporu. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:choppers_hat + 14 credits.

11. The desert conspiracy

Vivi’s homeland is being driven into civil war. Break through Mr. 1 while assembling relief supplies.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter sequence: minepiece:bon_clay, mineminenomi:mr_1. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 14 credits.

12. Rain over Alabasta

Crocodile is Baroque Works’ Mr. 0. Face the same character through Mine Mine no Mi’s boss encounter; this closes the Alabasta retelling.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter sequence: mineminenomi:mr_0. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.

13. Dreamers on Jaya

The search for a sky island leads to Jaya. Challenge Bellamy and prepare for the Knock Up Stream route.

Destination: jaya. Encounter sequence: minepiece:bellamy_grand_line. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:white_wool. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 16 credits.

14. A bell above the clouds

Reach Skypiea through the world’s sky route. Enel’s lightning threatens both sky people and Shandians.

Destination: skypiea. Encounter sequence: minepiece:enel. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 16 credits.

15. The Davy Back challenge

Foxy’s contests threaten the crew’s unity. This combat encounter adapts that contest; it does not transfer ownership of your multiplayer crew.

Destination: long_ring_long_land. Encounter sequence: minepiece:foxy. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 16 credits.

16. Shipwrights of Water 7

A damaged ship and Robin’s disappearance split the crew. Deliver repair materials to the contact while searching Water 7.

Destination: water_7. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:usopps_overall + 18 credits.

17. The gates of justice

The crew pursues Robin to Enies Lobby. Break the outer defense by facing Blueno.

Destination: enies_lobby. Encounter sequence: minepiece:blueno_cp_9. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:kuro_kabuto + 18 credits.

18. I want to live

Robin’s rescue culminates in the fight with Rob Lucci. Defeat his CP9 encounter and provide provisions for the escape.

Destination: enies_lobby. Encounter sequence: grandline:spandam, minepiece:kalifa_cp_9, minepiece:jabra_cp_9, minepiece:kaku_cp_9, minepiece:lucci_cp_9. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 18 credits.

19. A sword in the fog

Shadows are stolen on Thriller Bark. Challenge the zombie swordsman Ryuma in this retelling of the island’s battles.

Destination: thriller_bark. Encounter sequence: minepiece:ryuma. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:shusui + 20 credits.

20. The master of shadows

Moria commands the island’s stolen shadows. Face him before leaving the Florian Triangle.

Destination: thriller_bark. Encounter sequence: minepiece:oars, minepiece:gecko_moria. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 20 credits.

21. Broken bubbles

The first visit to Sabaody ends in separation. Face a Pacifista as a combat retelling; do not treat victory as rewriting Kuma’s role in the story.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter sequence: mineminenomi:pacifista. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 4 × minecraft:diamond + 20 credits.

22. An island of warriors

Luffy reaches Amazon Lily after the separation. Your expedition retraces that route and prepares supplies for the rescue voyage.

Destination: amazon_lily. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 22 credits.

23. Below the prison gates

Impel Down is a rescue journey, not the defeat of Magellan. Chart the prison destination and bring escape supplies. A custom Magellan combat trial adapts the danger for gameplay; defeating this trial does not claim a canonical victory over him.

Destination: nether_gateway. Encounter sequence: grandline:magellan. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 22 credits.

24. The cost of Marineford

The war brings irreversible losses in canon. Carry medical supplies and record the battlefield; this mission does not claim to save Ace or defeat every Admiral.

Destination: marineford. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:golden_carrot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:aces_hat + 22 credits.

25. Two years, one meeting place

Return to Sabaody after the story’s training interval. Reassemble provisions for the undersea crossing. No real-time two-year wait is imposed.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:luffys_shirt + 24 credits.

26. Ten thousand meters below

The route into the New World passes Fish-Man Island. Reach its underwater settlement and challenge Hody Jones.

Destination: fishman_island. Encounter sequence: minepiece:hody_jones. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 24 credits.

27. Fire and ice

Law’s alliance with Luffy takes shape on Punk Hazard. Confront Vergo and deliver supplies for the evacuation. Then confront Caesar in a custom gas encounter to finish the evacuation.

Destination: punk_hazard. Encounter sequence: minepiece:vergo, grandline:caesar. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:laws_hat + 24 credits.

28. The colosseum and the underground port

Dressrosa hides an arms trade. Challenge Burgess as a colosseum retelling, then report what the expedition found. Face Bellamy and Doflamingo next to break the operation behind the spectacle.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter sequence: minepiece:jesus_burgess, minepiece:bellamy_new_world, grandline:doflamingo. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 26 credits.

29. The road written in stone

On Zou, the crew learns the purpose of the Road Poneglyphs. Face Jack in a reconstruction of the raid, then help the recovering settlement. This task awards supplies, not an invented fifth Road Poneglyph.

Destination: zou. Encounter sequence: minepiece:jack. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 26 credits.

30. The seducing woods

Sanji’s retrieval takes the crew into Big Mom’s territory. Fight Cracker while provisioning the escape route.

Destination: whole_cake. Encounter sequence: minepiece:charlotte_perospero, minepiece:charlotte_cracker. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 26 credits.

31. A mirror-world duel

Luffy’s clash with Katakuri buys the crew time. The encounter uses the island’s surface clearing as a gameplay arena.

Destination: whole_cake. Encounter sequence: minepiece:charlotte_katakuri. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 28 credits.

32. The raid on Onigashima

The alliance moves against Kaido. Challenge King as the raid gathers strength, then prepare for the final encounter.

Destination: wano. Encounter sequence: grandline:orochi, minepiece:page_one, minepiece:ulti, minepiece:scratchmen_apoo, minepiece:queen, minepiece:king. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:enma + 28 credits.

33. The dawn over Wano

Face Kaido in the Wano climax. Use the combat and Haki systems you developed on the voyage; this is an endgame challenge.

Destination: wano. Encounter sequence: minepiece:charlotte_linlin_wano_country, minepiece:kaidou. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 28 credits.

34. The future island

The expedition reaches Egghead. Face Lucci’s CP0 incarnation and gather research notes. Later revelations remain outside this campaign’s narration.

Destination: egghead. Encounter sequence: minepiece:s_bear, minepiece:s_hawk, minepiece:s_snake, minepiece:s_shark, minepiece:kaku_cp_0, minepiece:lucci_cp_0. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 30 credits.

35. A horizon of giants

Reach Elbaph to close this expedition. The ongoing manga’s unresolved events and the nature of the One Piece are not invented here.

Destination: elbaph. Encounter sequence: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 16 × minecraft:diamond + 30 credits.

36. The prison shadow

A combat retelling of the prison crisis. Challenge Shiryu; this does not rewrite the escape or Magellan’s canonical outcome.

Destination: nether_gateway. Encounter sequence: minepiece:shiryu. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 30 credits.

37. Admirals at the summit

Optional endgame combat trials against the admirals. These are hypothetical duels; the Marineford story and its losses remain unchanged.

Destination: marineford. Encounter sequence: minepiece:aokiji, minepiece:kizaru, minepiece:akainu. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 32 credits.

38. The emperor’s pursuit

An optional Big Mom challenge in Totto Land. The original retrieval mission was an escape, not her defeat here.

Destination: whole_cake. Encounter sequence: minepiece:charlotte_linlin. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 32 credits.

39. The Blackbeard fleet

A hypothetical endgame assault on the Blackbeard fleet. This challenge invents no canonical ending for Teach.

Destination: hachinosu. Encounter sequence: minepiece:jesus_burgess, minepiece:van_augur, minepiece:vasco_shot, minepiece:shiryu, minepiece:marshall_d_teach. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 32 credits.

40. The Five Elders’ siege

A hypothetical five-stage trial against the Elders present at Egghead. These defeatable combat adaptations do not reproduce their canonical regeneration or assert that the crew defeated all five.

Destination: egghead. Encounter sequence: grandline:saturn, grandline:mars, grandline:warcury, grandline:ju_peter, minepiece:ethanbaron_v_nusjuro. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 34 credits.

41. The Holy Land’s hidden ruler

A clearly hypothetical endgame boss trial. Winning this fight is gameplay progression, not a claim about the manga’s ending.

Destination: mariejois. Encounter sequence: minepiece:imu. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 34 credits.

42. A knight in Elbaph

An optional combat interpretation using the installed Gunko model. The quest does not assert a final outcome for the ongoing arc.

Destination: elbaph. Encounter sequence: minepiece:gunko. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 34 credits.
