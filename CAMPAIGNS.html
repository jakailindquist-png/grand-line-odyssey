Campaign reference

Pirate — 35 chaptersMarine — 15 chaptersRevolutionary Army — 14 chaptersCelestial Dragon — 12 chapters

All dialogue and player assignments below are original fan adaptations. Canon events are context, not a claim that every manga scene is simulated. Spoilers through Wano and the arrival at Egghead/Elbaph.

Pirate — 35 chapters

Follow the Straw Hats’ main route from Dawn to Elbaph through exploration and boss retellings.

1. A sail from Dawn

Begin at Dawn Island. Luffy starts his journey in East Blue; your own crew follows that route without replacing him. Secure timber for a first ship.

Destination: starter_east_blue. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:oak_planks. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:straw_hat + 8 credits.

2. The axe over Shells Town

Morgan rules the base through fear. Investigate the town and challenge his story encounter, echoing the liberation of Zoro.

Destination: shells_town. Encounter: mineminenomi:morgan. Delivery: 8 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:zoros_shirt + 8 credits.

3. Orange Town under siege

Buggy threatens the settlement on the Organ Islands. Break his hold over the harbor in a retelling encounter.

Destination: organ_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:buggy. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 8 credits.

4. The liar and the captain

Usopp warns Syrup Village of a real threat. Face Kuro and help keep the village supplied.

Destination: gecko_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:kuro. Delivery: 12 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:kabuto + 10 credits.

5. A meal on the Baratie

The sea restaurant feeds the hungry, even dangerous strangers. Face Don Krieg after gathering provisions.

Destination: baratie. Encounter: mineminenomi:don_krieg. Delivery: 12 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:flintlock + 10 credits.

6. The promise at Arlong Park

Nami asks her friends for help. Challenge Arlong in the Conomi Islands and support the villages he exploited.

Destination: conomi_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:arlong. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

7. The town of the beginning and end

Loguetown stands on the Polestar Islands. Resupply before entering the Grand Line; the canon crew escapes Smoker here rather than defeating him.

Destination: polestar_islands. Encounter: none. Delivery: 8 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:sandai_kitetsu + 12 credits.

8. Across Reverse Mountain

Sail through the world mod’s Reverse Mountain route to Whisky Peak on Cactus Island. Baroque Works hides behind a welcoming harbor.

Destination: cactus_island. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_5. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 12 credits.

9. A hundred-year duel

Dorry and Brogy’s duel is interrupted by Baroque Works. Confront Mr. 3 in this prehistoric wilderness.

Destination: little_garden. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_3. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 12 credits.

10. Snow over Drum

Nami’s illness brings the crew to Drum. Help the island’s people and face Wapol, whose name is spelled Waporu in Mine Piece.

Destination: drum. Encounter: minepiece:waporu. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:choppers_hat + 14 credits.

11. The desert conspiracy

Vivi’s homeland is being driven into civil war. Break through Mr. 1 while assembling relief supplies.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_1. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 14 credits.

12. Rain over Alabasta

Crocodile is Baroque Works’ Mr. 0. Face the same character through Mine Mine no Mi’s boss encounter; this closes the Alabasta retelling.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_0. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.

13. Dreamers on Jaya

The search for a sky island leads to Jaya. Challenge Bellamy and prepare for the Knock Up Stream route.

Destination: jaya. Encounter: minepiece:bellamy_grand_line. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:white_wool. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:wooden_box + 16 credits.

14. A bell above the clouds

Reach Skypiea through the world’s sky route. Enel’s lightning threatens both sky people and Shandians.

Destination: skypiea. Encounter: minepiece:enel. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 16 credits.

15. The Davy Back challenge

Foxy’s contests threaten the crew’s unity. This combat encounter adapts that contest; it does not transfer ownership of your multiplayer crew.

Destination: long_ring_long_land. Encounter: minepiece:foxy. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 16 credits.

16. Shipwrights of Water 7

A damaged ship and Robin’s disappearance split the crew. Deliver repair materials to the contact while searching Water 7.

Destination: water_7. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:usopps_overall + 18 credits.

17. The gates of justice

The crew pursues Robin to Enies Lobby. Break the outer defense by facing Blueno.

Destination: enies_lobby. Encounter: minepiece:blueno_cp_9. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:kuro_kabuto + 18 credits.

18. I want to live

Robin’s rescue culminates in the fight with Rob Lucci. Defeat his CP9 encounter and provide provisions for the escape.

Destination: enies_lobby. Encounter: minepiece:lucci_cp_9. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 18 credits.

19. A sword in the fog

Shadows are stolen on Thriller Bark. Challenge the zombie swordsman Ryuma in this retelling of the island’s battles.

Destination: thriller_bark. Encounter: minepiece:ryuma. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:shusui + 20 credits.

20. The master of shadows

Moria commands the island’s stolen shadows. Face him before leaving the Florian Triangle.

Destination: thriller_bark. Encounter: minepiece:gecko_moria. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 20 credits.

21. Broken bubbles

The first visit to Sabaody ends in separation. Face a Pacifista as a combat retelling; do not treat victory as rewriting Kuma’s role in the story.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter: mineminenomi:pacifista. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 4 × minecraft:diamond + 20 credits.

22. An island of warriors

Luffy reaches Amazon Lily after the separation. Your expedition retraces that route and prepares supplies for the rescue voyage.

Destination: amazon_lily. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 22 credits.

23. Below the prison gates

Impel Down is a rescue journey, not the defeat of Magellan. Chart the prison destination and bring escape supplies.

Destination: nether_gateway. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 22 credits.

24. The cost of Marineford

The war brings irreversible losses in canon. Carry medical supplies and record the battlefield; this mission does not claim to save Ace or defeat every Admiral.

Destination: marineford. Encounter: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:golden_carrot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:aces_hat + 22 credits.

25. Two years, one meeting place

Return to Sabaody after the story’s training interval. Reassemble provisions for the undersea crossing. No real-time two-year wait is imposed.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:luffys_shirt + 24 credits.

26. Ten thousand meters below

The route into the New World passes Fish-Man Island. Reach its underwater settlement and challenge Hody Jones.

Destination: fishman_island. Encounter: minepiece:hody_jones. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 24 credits.

27. Fire and ice

Law’s alliance with Luffy takes shape on Punk Hazard. Confront Vergo and deliver supplies for the evacuation. Caesar is not a scripted boss in this build.

Destination: punk_hazard. Encounter: minepiece:vergo. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:laws_hat + 24 credits.

28. The colosseum and the underground port

Dressrosa hides an arms trade. Challenge Burgess as a colosseum retelling, then report what the expedition found. Doflamingo’s final duel is not implemented.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter: minepiece:jesus_burgess. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 26 credits.

29. The road written in stone

On Zou, the crew learns the purpose of the Road Poneglyphs. Help the recovering settlement. This task awards supplies, not an invented fifth Road Poneglyph.

Destination: zou. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 26 credits.

30. The seducing woods

Sanji’s retrieval takes the crew into Big Mom’s territory. Fight Cracker while provisioning the escape route.

Destination: whole_cake. Encounter: minepiece:charlotte_cracker. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 26 credits.

31. A mirror-world duel

Luffy’s clash with Katakuri buys the crew time. The encounter uses the island’s surface clearing as a gameplay arena.

Destination: whole_cake. Encounter: minepiece:charlotte_katakuri. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 28 credits.

32. The raid on Onigashima

The alliance moves against Kaido. Challenge King as the raid gathers strength, then prepare for the final encounter.

Destination: wano. Encounter: minepiece:king. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:enma + 28 credits.

33. The dawn over Wano

Face Kaido in the Wano climax. Use the combat and Haki systems you developed on the voyage; this is an endgame challenge.

Destination: wano. Encounter: minepiece:kaidou. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 28 credits.

34. The future island

The expedition reaches Egghead. Face Lucci’s CP0 incarnation and gather research notes. Later revelations remain outside this campaign’s narration.

Destination: egghead. Encounter: minepiece:lucci_cp_0. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 30 credits.

35. A horizon of giants

Reach Elbaph to close this expedition. The ongoing manga’s unresolved events and the nature of the One Piece are not invented here.

Destination: elbaph. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 16 × minecraft:diamond + 30 credits.

Marine — 15 chapters

An original Marine commission around canon crises: piracy, corruption, relief and intelligence.

1. Enlistment at the branch

As an original Marine recruit, inspect the branch and deliver its ration allotment. Your service runs beside the canon voyage.

Destination: shells_town. Encounter: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_hat + 8 credits.

2. Piracy off Orange Town

Buggy’s raid gives the branch an urgent assignment. Protect the harbor through a boss retelling.

Destination: organ_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:buggy. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_shirt + 8 credits.

3. Evidence at Syrup Village

Investigate Kuro’s concealed piracy instead of trusting appearances. File the evidence after the encounter.

Destination: gecko_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:kuro. Delivery: 12 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_pants + 8 credits.

4. The blockade at Baratie

Krieg’s fleet threatens shipping. Break the attack and ensure civilians receive supplies.

Destination: baratie. Encounter: mineminenomi:don_krieg. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_boots + 10 credits.

5. A corrupt arrangement

Arlong’s reign exposes failures within local Marine administration. Confront the threat and document those failures.

Destination: conomi_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:arlong. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:flintlock + 10 credits.

6. Loguetown watch

Join the port watch under the shadow of Smoker’s pursuit. Service here is an original assignment, not promotion into a canon character’s job.

Destination: polestar_islands. Encounter: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

7. Desert intelligence

Baroque Works has manipulated a kingdom. Investigate its armed network and confront Mr. 1.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_1. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_captain_cape + 12 credits.

8. The Warlord’s secret

Crocodile’s status does not erase his crimes. Face the Mr. 0 encounter and record the evidence for headquarters.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_0. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 12 credits.

9. Water 7 logistics

Survey shipyard capacity and deliver repair supplies before the crisis at Enies Lobby.

Destination: water_7. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 12 credits.

10. After the Buster Call

Your orders concern survivors and damage reports, not a fictional victory over the Straw Hats. Inspect the island and deliver rations.

Destination: enies_lobby. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 6 × minecraft:diamond + 14 credits.

11. Mobilization at Marineford

Prepare the headquarters for the war. The player’s logistics assignment does not reverse the deaths recorded in canon.

Destination: marineford. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:smoker_jacket + 14 credits.

12. A New World posting

G-5 operates far from the comfort of headquarters. Outfit an expedition toward Punk Hazard.

Destination: g_5_island. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 14 credits.

13. The traitor at G-5

Vergo’s infiltration threatens the Marines themselves. Face him in an investigation-inspired retelling.

Destination: punk_hazard. Encounter: minepiece:vergo. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 16 credits.

14. Justice in Dressrosa

Inspect the aftermath of the underground arms trade. Choose between local relief and career influence when filing the report.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 16 credits.

15. A report to New Marineford

Complete your commission with intelligence from both halves of the Grand Line. Your character’s standing is original progression, not a canon Admiral appointment.

Destination: new_marineford. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 16 × minecraft:diamond + 16 credits.

Revolutionary Army — 14 chapters

An original liberation network around Tequila Wolf, Baltigo, Dressrosa and Mary Geoise.

1. Quiet work in the Goa Kingdom

Begin as an original Revolutionary courier on Dawn Island. Gather provisions for people beyond the kingdom’s privileged districts.

Destination: starter_east_blue. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:pipe + 8 credits.

2. The occupied coast

Arlong’s coercion cuts villages off from help. Face him while building your first relief network.

Destination: conomi_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:arlong. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 8 credits.

3. The bridge at Tequila Wolf

The Revolutionary Army liberates people forced to build this bridge. Your original mission supplies an evacuation route.

Destination: tequila_wolf. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:sabos_hat + 8 credits.

4. Rendezvous on Baltigo

Report to the Revolutionary Army’s former headquarters and deliver field notes. The map contains locations from different periods in the story.

Destination: baltigo. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

5. The record of Ohara

Ohara’s destruction is part of the world’s history. Gather notes at its fan-built representation without claiming to undo the Buster Call.

Destination: starter_west_blue. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 10 credits.

6. Sorbet contacts

Sorbet is central to Kuma’s history. Your courier line supports residents; it does not recreate or rewrite every event in his life.

Destination: sorbet_kingdom. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

7. The desert’s supply lines

A manufactured civil war feeds suffering. Disrupt Baroque Works and help keep relief routes open.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_1. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 12 credits.

8. The human cost of Sabaody

The archipelago exposes the slave trade protected by the World Nobles. Deliver escape supplies to an original contact.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 8 × minecraft:diamond + 12 credits.

9. The network at Momoiro

Kamabakka Kingdom on Momoiro becomes a Revolutionary meeting point. Deliver provisions and reconnect your scattered network.

Destination: momoiro_island. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:cooked_beef. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 12 credits.

10. Evidence from Punk Hazard

Track the chain of manufactured weapons toward the underworld. Confront Vergo while protecting your evidence.

Destination: punk_hazard. Encounter: minepiece:vergo. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.

11. An underground port

Sabo and his allies investigate arms exports from Dressrosa. Your original courier task carries evidence and relief supplies from that operation.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:kuro_kabuto + 14 credits.

12. A rival in the colosseum

Burgess seeks power amid Dressrosa’s upheaval. This encounter is a combat adaptation, not a claim that the player replaces Sabo.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter: minepiece:jesus_burgess. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.

13. The Holy Land operation

The Revolutionary Army challenges the World Nobles at Mary Geoise. Your role is to stage supplies and report; the manga’s full rescue sequence is not simulated.

Destination: mariejois. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:golden_carrot. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 16 credits.

14. The next safe harbor

Return with the operation’s records. Your network continues through contact trust, local goodwill and rival encounters.

Destination: momoiro_island. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 16 × minecraft:diamond + 16 credits.

Celestial Dragon — 12 chapters

An original World Noble household campaign about privilege, public consequences and political standing.

1. A house commission

Play an original member of a World Noble household traveling under commission. Noble status is inherited in canon; this is not a Marine promotion tree.

Destination: starter_east_blue. Encounter: none. Delivery: 8 × minecraft:gold_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:celestial_dragon_bubble + 8 credits.

2. An escort report

Inspect a Marine branch that provides security along the route. Local goodwill and household influence will track how you handle your commission.

Destination: shells_town. Encounter: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:marine_captain_cape + 8 credits.

3. A pirate interruption

Buggy threatens a port used by the household’s suppliers. Resolve the encounter before filing your account.

Destination: organ_islands. Encounter: mineminenomi:buggy. Delivery: 0 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:flintlock + 8 credits.

4. The price of passage

The Baratie serves travelers regardless of rank. Supply a meal and decide whether your report emphasizes relief or household prestige.

Destination: baratie. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:cooked_cod. Reward: 16 × minecraft:gold_ingot + 10 credits.

5. A kingdom’s testimony

Investigate a member kingdom’s supply disruption during the Baroque Works crisis. A noble seal does not reveal every hidden conspiracy.

Destination: alabasta. Encounter: mineminenomi:mr_1. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:iron_box + 10 credits.

6. Shipyard commissions

Water 7 supplies skilled builders. Complete a shipyard delivery for the household’s vessel.

Destination: water_7. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:iron_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 10 credits.

7. Privilege at Sabaody

World Nobles use their protection at Sabaody to impose their will. This original household story lets your choices build influence or local goodwill; it does not rewrite Charlos’s canon actions.

Destination: sabaody. Encounter: none. Delivery: 16 × minecraft:gold_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:celestial_dragon_bubble + 12 credits.

8. The Holy Land audience

Return to the World Nobles’ home atop the Red Line. Deliver your commission records and choose how the household will remember your service.

Destination: mariejois. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 8 × minecraft:diamond + 12 credits.

9. War reports from headquarters

Marineford’s war shifts the balance of power. Gather reports and fund supplies without inventing a different canonical outcome.

Destination: marineford. Encounter: none. Delivery: 24 × minecraft:gold_ingot. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 12 credits.

10. An unsettled New World

The fall of underworld arrangements exposes fragile political ties. Carry records from Dressrosa for your house.

Destination: dressrosa. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:paper. Reward: 1 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.

11. A threatened authority

Revolutionary action reaches the Holy Land. Your original assignment organizes emergency stores and records how you treat those under your protection.

Destination: mariejois. Encounter: none. Delivery: 48 × minecraft:bread. Reward: 12 × minecraft:diamond + 14 credits.

12. The household’s legacy

Close your commission with a final allocation of resources. Authority, trust and local goodwill persist; this path does not grant unlimited command over every NPC.

Destination: mariejois. Encounter: none. Delivery: 32 × minecraft:gold_ingot. Reward: 2 × mineminenomi:golden_box + 14 credits.
