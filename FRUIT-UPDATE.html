Keep your world. Update your existing 0.5.0 instance.

Already playing Grand Line Odyssey 0.5.0? This is an in-place addon update. The 37 third-party downloads, Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.4.0 are identical in 0.5.0 and 0.5.1. Your world, character, crew, controls and other mods stay in the same instance.

  1. Close Minecraft. Back up your existing instance’s complete saves folder, including data and serverconfig.
  2. Download 0.5.0 → 0.5.1 update JAR

    In Prism, right-click your existing Grand Line Odyssey 0.5.0 instance and open Edit → Mods. Remove only grandline-odyssey-0.5.0.jar, then choose Add File and select the downloaded grandline-odyssey-0.5.1.jar. Keep one Odyssey JAR.

  3. Launch that same instance and open your current world. The new sailing setting is created automatically. Your saved quest progress remains; already completed quests are not paid again.
  4. Have the host and every friend make the same JAR update before joining. Press O for quests and J for abilities. Read the update details →

Upgrading from 0.3.0 or earlier? Download the full 0.5.1 pack It contains the wildlife dependencies added in 0.4.0; import it as a new instance and move your backed-up world.

Eat. Select. Use.

Hold right-click to eat a supported fruit when you have no existing fruit power. The first selected move may be a basic attack; cycle to your fruit’s named techniques.

Default keyMine Piece fruit control
JOpen abilities, or finish character creation.
G / Shift+GNext / previous move.
Hold VUse or charge the selected move. Release to finish a held action.
NTransform or change form where supported.

Rebind these under Options → Controls → Grand Line Odyssey. Your energy, selected move and cost appear above the hotbar.

/voyage abilities
For Mine Mine no Mi fruits, J opens that mod’s ability menu. Equip unlocked abilities there and use its combat mode and ability slots. G/V/N apply to Mine Piece fruits.

Find a fruit. Grow into its powers.

Fruit broker

Choose from 40 named items. Each costs 64 supply credits + 8 diamonds. Clicking a broker entry prepares a purchase command; press Enter to buy.

/voyage fruits

Fruit training

Spend 16 supply credits to add 200 maximum energy, up to 4,000. Starting capacity is at least 400. Training does not refill energy.

/voyage trainfruit

Something still not working?

The abilities menu does not open

Use the command below. If your character is incomplete, choose Race, Faction and Fighting Style, then Finish. Check the Grand Line Odyssey key bindings for conflicts.

/voyage setup
I cannot eat another fruit

One fruit power per character. The bridge keeps your existing power and blocks a second fruit across both mods. Try another power in a separate test world. The generic debug/reset item called “Devil Fruit” is excluded; use a named fruit.

Some techniques are missing

Move availability follows energy capacity and native requirements such as forms or equipment. Training opens stronger moves. Some upstream fruits have limited implementations: Mine Piece’s Ope fruit, for example, has a small move set.

Before eating a duplicate fruit, you can exchange it for its matching Mine Mine no Mi item. Hold it in your main hand and use:

/voyage convertfruit

The exchange consumes one item and gives one counterpart; it does not change an eaten power. Fruits without a matching counterpart are kept.

What is included and what was tested?

The existing 71 Mine Mine no Mi fruit items remain. The 40 Mine Piece items include alternate versions of some of the same fruits, not 40 entirely new canonical powers. Some powers are mod-author interpretations.
